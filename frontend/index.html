Steel-and-glass pavilion, olive trees & lantern stone courtyard

Framed by heirloom olive trees, climbing jasmine, and heated limestone floors, The Juniper Glasshouse is an architectural conservatory built for luminous ceremonies and candlelit black-tie dinners under the stars.

A Seamless Celebration

Every moment considered, from morning to last dance.

01 / Morning Arrival

Private Preparation Suites

Settle in from 10:00 AM in our sunlit Bridal Cottage and Groom's Parlor with espresso service, garment steamers, and private garden courtyards for first-look portraits.

02 / Golden Hour

Ceremony & Courtyard Cocktails

Exchange vows beneath the creekside sycamores or inside the botanical conservatory, then gather on the lantern-lit terrace for seasonal hors d'oeuvres and signature cocktails.

03 / Candlelit Reception

Heirloom Dining & Dancing

Dine at handcrafted oak banquet tables with curated local culinary partners and full in-house bar hospitality, followed by dancing under the timber trusses or glass roof.

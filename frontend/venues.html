Venue 01 • Creekside Estate

Hollow Brook Barn

Nestled along a spring-fed limestone creek, Hollow Brook Barn features 28-foot reclaimed cedar timber trusses, heated and cooled interiors, a wrap-around covered veranda, and a private bridal cottage.

  • Guest Capacity 180 Seated Dining • 220 Cocktail Reception
  • Evening Music Outdoor amplified music concludes at 10:00 PM; indoor reception continues until 11:00 PM.
  • Connectivity & Comfort Full climate control, 50 Mbps fiber Wi-Fi & backup generator power.
  • On-Site Parking Private estate lot (85 spaces) with step-free drop-off circle.

Venue 02 • Botanical Conservatory

The Juniper Glasshouse

An architectural steel-and-glass conservatory bathed in natural light by day and warm lantern glow by night, framed by olive trees and heated limestone floors.

  • Guest Capacity 96 Seated Dining • 120 Standing Cocktail Reception
  • Guest Parking Adjacent Emerson Garage at $8 per vehicle evening rate.
  • Climate & Acoustics Radiant floor heating, UV-filtered glass roof & integrated house audio.
  • Catering & Bar Curated culinary partners + full licensed conservatory bar.

Planning Details

Frequently asked questions.

Contact Us
Hollow Brook Barn accommodates up to 180 guests for seated dining and up to 220 guests for a cocktail-style reception. The Juniper Glasshouse accommodates up to 96 guests for seated dining and up to 120 guests for a standing reception.
Guests at The Juniper Glasshouse park at the adjacent Emerson Garage, which offers a flat $8 per vehicle evening rate after 5:00 PM, with step-free elevator access to the conservatory courtyard.
In accordance with Tennessee licensing, BYO alcohol is not permitted at either venue; all alcoholic beverages are served by Juniper Hollow's licensed bar team. At Hollow Brook Barn, licensed outside caterers are welcome with a $600 outside caterer fee.
Courtesy holds are complimentary for 72 hours following your tour or consultation. To officially reserve a wedding date on our calendar, a signed event agreement and a 30% retainer are required.
